The romfs files in the release archives are edited copies of Tears of the Kingdom 1.2.1 data files
(interface sound bank, RSDB tables, save-data schema, English text archive, effect and sound link
tables, effect file list, actor packs, models, inventory icons and the resource-size table). The
module, texture sidecars and sound bank also carry content converted from Breath of the
Wild 1.5.0, and the two wheel-page icons are converted from Breath of the Wild 1.6.0 inventory
icons. The Champions' Blessings carry content converted from Breath of the Wild 1.6.0: their
sounds and voice cues, the Champion item names, descriptions and icons, and Gale's crouch pose.
Their effects are recolored copies of Tears of the Kingdom effects. They only work with a
legally owned copy of the game and are not relicensed. Native builds use devkitPro/devkitA64.

The supplied native sphere data is the donor for the bomb models/materials, effect bank and
explosion routing; the cube adapts that route using its own geometry and texture maps.
Third-party and game-derived assets retain their original rights and are not MIT licensed.
