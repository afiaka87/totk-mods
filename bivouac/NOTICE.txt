The romfs files in the release archives are edited copies of Tears of the Kingdom 1.2.1 data files
(UI layout archive, save-data schema, English text archive, two actor tables, one actor pack and the
resource-size table). They only work with a legally owned copy of the game. Native builds use
devkitPro/devkitA64.
