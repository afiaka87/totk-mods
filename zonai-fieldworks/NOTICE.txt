The module is compiled from Fieldworks' own source and from the source of its eight members
(Phantom Foothold, Zonai Hookshot with Arrowbound, Fleet Sync, Material Columns, BotW Abilities,
Bivouac, Self Recall and Zonai Survey). Each member's notice in its own folder applies to its part.

The romfs files in the release archive are the members' release files, combined:
- Edited copies of Tears of the Kingdom 1.2.1 data files (UI layout archive, save-data schema,
  English text archive, RSDB tables, effect and sound link tables, interface sound bank, actor
  packs, models, inventory icons and the resource-size table).
- Content converted from the owner's Breath of the Wild 1.5.0 and 1.6.0 dumps for BotW Abilities:
  Wolf Link's model, skeleton, animations and sounds, the Champions' sounds, voice cues, item
  names, descriptions and icons, and the wheel-page icons.
- Zonai Survey's compact locale packs with item names, collectible icons and subset font artwork.
- Material Columns' four small quick-menu layout derivatives and its first-party label and
  category files.
- Eight wheel icons made for Fieldworks.
- `romfs/Fieldworks/Merge`: the parts of the edited data files above that are not in the game's
  originals (added rows, save flags, tags, names, sounds and memory budgets), in Fieldworks' own
  formats.

Game-derived and third-party assets retain their original rights and are not MIT licensed. They
only work with a legally owned copy of the game. Native builds use devkitPro/devkitA64.
